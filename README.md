# My favorite series is :heart_eyes: **Bagito** :couplekiss:
- **Directed by:** Onat A. Diaz and Jojo A. Saguin
- **Cast:** Nash Aguas, Alexa Ilacad and Ella Cruz
- **Release date:**	November 17, 2014 to March 13, 2015
- **Plot:** Drew (Nash Aguas) is a teenager forced to face the responsibilities of being a young father because of his huge mistake. How will Drew's newly born child change his life and the lives of his loved ones? Is he ready to leave his childhood behind and face the difficulties of being a young father? How will Drew's parents help and guide him as he enters the "real world"?

## I like this series because of:
1. Nash Aguas' excellent acting
2. its relatable scenes
3. how it tackles prominent problems in the Philippines
